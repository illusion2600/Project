SHORT WALKTHROUGH
Campus Pantry connects food-collection bookings, attendance, volunteer staffing and stock records through a Python command-line application and PostgreSQL.
The database has 16 tables and three reporting views. Twelve named SQL query groups produce thirteen SELECT result sets because Q8 has two. Person stores identity once, while student, volunteer and staff roles can overlap.
Students reserve collection sessions. A standard booking is limited to one active booking per student per day, with at most 50 non-cancelled bookings per session. A staff-attributed override needs a reason and never bypasses capacity. Booking transactions lock the student and session records to protect these decisions from concurrent requests.
Staff create a day of three shifts and twelve half-hour sessions. Shifts progress from PLANNED to OPEN to CLOSED. Opening requires one active main supervisor and one active assistant. Staffing is fixed after opening; completed inventory is protected after closure.
Attendance is separate from reservation. Staff enter a booking token during 10:00 inclusive to 16:00 exclusive, Australia/Sydney, on its booking date. Same-day late arrival remains valid even if the student's original shift has already closed. Repeating a valid scan returns the existing attendance result. No-shows are marked only at or after the operating day ends.
Volunteers request shifts and staff approve or reject pending requests. A later decision cannot overwrite a request that has already been reviewed.
Inventory requires a genuine opening observation before adjustments or a closing count. Estimated dispensing is opening plus receipts minus waste minus closing. The final demonstration checks 100 + 10 - 2 - 88 = 20 rice packs. These are synthetic demonstration quantities, not measured real-world demand.

QUESTIONS TO PREPARE
Why separate bookings and attendance?
A reservation does not prove collection occurred. The booking status and attendance timestamp record the actual check-in.

Why Person plus role tables?
It avoids repeated identity details and allows a person to hold multiple roles. Each role's primary key also references Person.

What makes an operation atomic?
One transaction commits all its database changes on success and rolls them back on failure. For example, a closing observation and its shift summary change together.

Is every relation in BCNF?
Do not make a blanket claim. stock_batch retains product_id even though purchase_line_id determines it. The final design documents that redundancy and protects the matching pair with a composite foreign key.

Does the application authenticate real students, scan QR images or send email?
No. The local showcase selects a synthetic UniKey identity and uses terminal token entry. University authentication, QR-image generation, camera scanning and email delivery are not integrated.

Are all records immutable?
No. Closed-shift inventory and opened-shift staffing are protected by the implemented rules. This is not a tamper-proof audit ledger. Stock adjustments aggregate by shift, batch and kind and retain the latest reason.
